## Overview

This repository holds my personal notes on a ledger I found while working nights as an archivist. It is not a project, a product or a tutorial. It is a place to keep what I know, in case the places I used before stop being safe.

## Background

I work the night shift at an old records office. Last month, behind a row of shelves near the boiler room, I found a ledger that is not in any catalogue.

The entries look ordinary until you read them twice. Payments go to names that do not appear in any register. Some dates fall after the building was closed to the public. Three columns of figures are missing, and several pages have been cut out cleanly with a blade.

I posted a short summary on R3ddit, in r/basement. The thread was removed within hours under rule 3 (no leaks). Since then my account has been watched, replies have vanished, and one moderator told me, politely, to stop.

So I started writing things down somewhere else. This is that place.
